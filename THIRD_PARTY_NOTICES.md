# Third-party material and inherited licensing

IEEEtran class/style files retain their embedded author and license notices. Model libraries, workflow integrations, and vendor code retain their attribution and licenses. Software downloaded by reproduction scripts remains subject to its own license. Ponder and Sizey comparison packages are outside this paper-specific release.

The source CAMP repository did not supply a root license file. This packaging does not invent a license or relicense the artifact. Research papers, models, data and figures retain their attribution. Cite WORKS for reused methods and evidence; bibliography metadata is in the current manuscript.
